# Project STEMulate: slogans and website messages

Brainstorm for the refreshed website. "Making STEM stick" is retired.
★ marks the option currently used on the site. Swap any of them by editing the
matching page; each one appears in exactly one place.

## Main slogan (hero, social bios, deck cover)

1. ★ **Turning every "why?" into "wow!"**
2. ~~STIM~~**STEM**ulating young minds (the crossed-out STIM, as a wordmark gag)
3. STEMpowering every child to discover their potential
4. Curiosity has no price tag.
5. Big questions. Small hands. Endless possibilities.
6. Level up your curiosity.
7. Press play on STEM.
8. Learn it. Build it. Play it.
9. Every child is a scientist. We just hand them the goggles.
10. STEM for every child, not just some.
11. Where play becomes discovery.
12. Sparking curious minds, one experiment at a time.

Top three: **1** for the website hero (short, child-friendly, works as a
sticker), **2** for the deck cover and logo lock-up (the crossed-out joke only
works visually), **3** for grant applications and partner emails (says the
mission out loud).
