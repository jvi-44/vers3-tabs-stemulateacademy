Volunteer with us

Students who love STEM: help plan and run our in-person workshops, 1-to-1 sessions and learning journeys. Any enthusiastic youth volunteer who is passionate about STEM and eager to make a positive difference in the lives of underprivileged children and their families is welcome to join.

We also recruit student volunteers for curriculum, graphics, dev, operations and partnerships every year.

Partner with us

Centres and organisations: bring our free workshops to the children you support. We run them in person with student care centres, family service centres, libraries and charities, and we'll send our curriculum packages and session details.

Partners can also bring STEMulate Academy, our new online add-on, to their children, so the learning carries on between sessions.

Start a STEMulate chapter

A STEMulate chapter is a branch of Project STEMulate that bridges the gap in STEM access where you live. Students aged 15 and above can apply on their own, as a group or as a school club.

Chapters join our international network to share resources and work across countries, and each gets a STEMulate mentor and our full resource library to help it get started.

  1. PrepareFind a few like-minded friends. If your chapter will be based in a school, get your school's approval.
  2. ApplyEvery team member sends the application form below. You'll hear back within a week.
  3. InterviewOnce your application is approved, we'll schedule a chat about you and your chapter plans.
