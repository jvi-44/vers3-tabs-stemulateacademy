About us

Started by students. Built for every child.

Project STEMulate is a ground-up, youth-led community of volunteers. We bring free, hands-on STEM to primary schoolers aged 7 to 12 from less-privileged backgrounds, so that curiosity never depends on what a family can afford.

Our story

Curiosity has no price tag.

In 2023, a group of Raffles Institution students noticed that many children from disadvantaged families miss out on the experiments, coding clubs and science trips their peers take for granted. So we started running our own.

Our workshops turn games children already love into science and maths lessons. When children kept asking for “one more video” or “one more game” after the session ended, we built STEMulate Academy so the learning could carry on at home.
