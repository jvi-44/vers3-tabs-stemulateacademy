About us

Started by students. Built for every child.

Project STEMulate is a student-initiated volunteering programme that runs STEM enrichment for primary school students from less-fortunate families in Singapore.

Our mission

To empower underprivileged children by inculcating an interest in STEM, fostering curiosity and confidence, and guiding them in discovering their own potential, bringing about positive change to society.

With a passion for STEM and a drive to make a difference in the lives of under-served children and their families, a group of Raffles Institution students formed Project STEMulate in 2023. We tackle educational inequality in Singapore by giving primary schoolers from disadvantaged backgrounds equal learning opportunities in STEM.

From one partner and our first workshops to ten partner organisations and an online academy.

Start a STEMulate chapter

A STEMulate chapter is a branch of Project STEMulate that bridges the gap in STEM access where you live. Students aged 15 and above can apply on their own, as a group or as a school club.

Chapters join our international network to share resources and work across countries, and each gets a STEMulate mentor and our full resource library to help it get started.

  1. PrepareFind a few like-minded friends. If your chapter will be based in a school, get your school's approval.
  2. ApplyEvery team member fills in the chapter application form. You'll hear back within a week.
  3. InterviewOnce your application is approved, we'll schedule a chat about you and your chapter plans.

Questions we get asked

What is Project STEMulate?

A student-initiated volunteering programme that carries out STEM enrichment activities for primary school students from less-fortunate families.

Who can volunteer?

Any enthusiastic youth volunteers who are passionate about STEM and eager to make a positive difference in the lives of underprivileged children and their families are welcome to join.

What kinds of activities do you run?

Hands-on workshops, interactive experiments, personal 1-to-1 sessions, learning journeys and a range of events, all designed to get children exploring STEM.

How often are the activities held?

1-to-1 sessions run weekly. Other event dates are on this website and in our monthly newsletter.

I have another question.

Email us at proj.stemulate@gmail.com and we'll get back to you within a few days.
