Evaluation

How we measure what matters

Headcounts only tell part of the story. We also track what children learn, how they feel about STEM, and whether partners and volunteers want to come back.

  1. Pre & post quizzesSame 5 questions before and after each workshop
  2. Smiley-face surveysKid-friendly 1–5 faces on enjoyment, interest and confidence
  3. Partner feedbackA short form for centre staff after every series
  4. Volunteer reflectionsHours logged and a quick reflection after each session
  5. Academy dataSign-ups, lessons finished and game high scores
