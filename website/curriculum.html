Curriculum · STEM x Games

From the games kids play to the science they'll use.

Three workshops, each built around a game children already love. Every session mixes a story, a bit of theory and a lot of hands-on making, and every topic links back to the primary school syllabus.

How a workshop runs

Six steps, one big “wow!”

Each in-person workshop runs for 1.5 to 3 hours and follows the same rhythm, so children always know what's coming next.

  1. Hello, STEM!Name tags, ice-breakers and what S, T, E and M stand for.
  2. Story timeThe STEMbots get into a pickle that only STEM can solve.
  3. Ground rulesBe kind, help your friends, join in.
  4. TheoryShort, picture-heavy explanations with quick games like Hangman and Kahoot.
  5. Hands-onBuild, test and play: rockets, terrariums, board games.
  6. ReflectWhat did we learn? Then the fun continues in STEMulate Academy.

Workshop 1 · Minecraft P5P6

Minecraft Masterminds

One hot summer afternoon, Sophia's garden starts to change. Children explore Minecraft biomes to learn how matter and water cycle, then measure blocks to build better.

States of matterThe water cycleEcosystemsAreaVolume of cubes & cuboids
Workshop slides 1 / 10

Children will be able to…

  • Name the three states of matter and how water changes between them
  • Explain where rain comes from: evaporation, condensation, precipitation
  • Compare land, water and mixed ecosystems
  • Find area with length × width, and by splitting odd shapes into rectangles
  • Work out the volume of cubes and cuboids

Hands-on activities

  • Bottle terrariumBuild a sealed mini ecosystem for a forest, desert or swamp biome, and see the water cycle keep it alive.
  • Minecraft paper-craftFold 3D blocks from nets, then calculate their volumes.

Workshop 2 · Monopoly P4P5P6

Mission Millionaire

What would you buy with a million dollars? Children learn to handle money like a property tycoon, then play Monopoly musical chairs across Singapore's MRT lines.

Money & decimalsGST (9%)Percentages & discountsBank interest
Workshop slides 1 / 10

Children will be able to…

  • Convert between dollars and cents
  • Add and subtract money, lining up the decimal points
  • Add 9% GST to a price
  • Work out discounts and sale prices with percentages
  • Explain how a bank pays interest on savings

Hands-on activities

  • Monopoly musical chairsEach chair is an MRT-stop property. When the music stops, decide whether to buy, add GST, apply discounts and pay rent.
  • SG Budget gameMake spending choices in groups and keep the budget balanced.

Workshop 3 · Among Us P5P6

Space Busters

The Skeld is drifting towards the Sun! Children learn how energy and forces move a spaceship, launch balloon rockets, then crack BODMAS tasks to find the impostor.

Forms of energyForcesHow rockets flyOrder of operations (BODMAS)
Workshop slides 1 / 10

Children will be able to…

  • Name kinetic, potential, light, sound and heat energy, with everyday examples
  • Trace how energy changes form in a bouncing ball and a light bulb
  • Describe gravity, friction, magnetic and spring forces
  • Explain thrust, drag and weight on a rocket
  • Solve mixed calculations in BODMAS order

Hands-on activities

  • Two-stage balloon rocketThread balloons onto a string, release both nozzles and watch thrust beat drag.
  • Among Us task huntTurn the room into the Skeld. Each crew task is a maths problem; solve them all to find the impostor.
