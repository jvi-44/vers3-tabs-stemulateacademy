Who we are

Started by students. Built for every child.

Project STEMulate was formed in 2023 by a group of Raffles Institution students with a passion for STEM and a drive to make a difference for under-served children and their families.

We tackle educational inequality in Singapore by giving primary schoolers from disadvantaged backgrounds equal learning opportunities in STEM, empowering them to break the poverty cycle.

Try a quiz question

Your Minecraft storage room is 4 blocks long, 3 blocks wide and 3 blocks tall. What is its volume?

36 cubic blocks. 4 × 3 × 3 = 36, the 3D space inside your storage room.

An item costs $50 with a 20% discount. What's the sale price?

$40. 20% of $50 is $10, so you pay $50 − $10.

What is 4 + 2 × (6 − 3)?

10. Brackets first (6 − 3 = 3), then multiply (2 × 3 = 6), then add (4 + 6).
