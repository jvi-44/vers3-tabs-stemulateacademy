All about our workshops

How a workshop runs

Each in-person workshop lasts 1.5 to 3 hours and follows the same rhythm, so children always know what's coming next.

  1. Hello, STEM!Name tags, ice-breakers and what S, T, E and M stand for.
  2. Story timeThe STEMbots get into a pickle that only STEM can solve.
  3. Ground rulesBe kind, help your friends, join in.
  4. TheoryShort, picture-heavy explanations with quick games like Hangman and Kahoot.
  5. Hands-onBuild, test and play: rockets, terrariums, board games.
  6. ReflectWhat did we learn? Then the fun continues in STEMulate Academy.

Workshops end. Curiosity doesn't.

STEMulate Academy is our online learning world, so the fun keeps going between sessions. Children sign in with a username and a 4-digit PIN, with no email needed, and their progress is saved to their account.

  • Story intros, video lessons and quizzes for each STEM x Games lesson
  • Games to play alone or live with friends
  • Atoms to spend on packs of phenomena and STEM-hero cards
  • A certificate at the end of every module

Started by students. Built for every child.

With a passion for STEM and a drive to make a difference in the lives of under-served children and their families, a group of Raffles Institution students formed Project STEMulate in 2023.

Our mission: to empower underprivileged children by inculcating an interest in STEM, fostering curiosity and confidence, and guiding them in discovering their own potential.
