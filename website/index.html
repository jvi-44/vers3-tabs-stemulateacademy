Hands on. Minds on.

Since 2023 our volunteers have run free STEM sessions in person with the children our partners support, in four ways.

  • STEM x workshops

    Hands-on workshops of 1.5 to 3 hours at our partner centres, each written by our own members.

  • Weekly 1-to-1 sessions

    Personal sessions every week. With VIVA Foundation, for children with cancer, since January 2025.

  • Learning journeys

    Trips that show children where STEM happens in the real world.

  • Monthly enrichment

    Regular activities with our partner centres, every month since 2024.

Six kinds of STEM x workshop

Each workshop pairs STEM with something children already love, with a theory part and a hands-on part, aligned with the Singapore primary school syllabus.

What changes for the children

The confidence and passion for STEM that grow in our sessions have begun to shape the children's long-term aspirations. Several have shared ambitions to pursue STEM careers.

We have also become not just mentors but peers in their lives. The children remember our first names and keep in touch with us outside lessons, and we are honoured to act as older sisters and role models.

“My children enjoyed going for Project STEMulate's workshops… afterwards they did talk about wanting to have a career in science… they were very inspired at the lesson!”

A mother of two participants

“After attending Project STEMulate's workshops, I am more encouraged to go for the Science stream in secondary school. I want to attend workshops about other topics of STEM!”

A participant, age 12

Started by students. Built for every child.

With a passion for STEM and a drive to make a difference in the lives of under-served children and their families, a group of Raffles Institution students formed Project STEMulate in 2023.

Our mission: to empower underprivileged children by inculcating an interest in STEM, fostering curiosity and confidence, and guiding them in discovering their own potential.
