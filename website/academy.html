STEMulate Academy · New in 2026

Workshops end. Curiosity doesn't.

STEMulate Academy is our new online add-on to the in-person workshops we have run since 2023, so children can keep exploring at home and between sessions.

Enter STEMulate Academy

How it fits with our workshops

Each Academy lesson matches a STEM x Games workshop. Children meet it at the end of a session, then carry on at home with the same STEMbots, topics and games.

  • Minecraft MastermindsMatter, the water cycle, area and volume. Games: Drippy's Water Cycle Dash and the Minecraft Room Designer.
  • Mission MillionaireMoney, GST, percentages and interest. Game: a Singapore-themed Monopoly.
  • Space BustersEnergy, forces, rockets and BODMAS. Games: Asteroid Blasters and Among Us tasks.

See the workshops these lessons follow

What's inside

  • Story intros, video lessons and quizzes for each STEM x Games lesson
  • Games to play alone or live with friends
  • Atoms to spend on packs of phenomena and STEM-hero cards
  • A certificate at the end of every module

For parents and centres

Simple and safe for primary schoolers.

  • No email neededChildren sign in with a username and a 4-digit PIN, and their progress is saved to their account.
  • Guests can look aroundAnyone can explore as a guest. Nothing a guest does is saved, and guests don't join the leaderboard or chat.
  • For centresBring the Academy to your children alongside our in-person workshops. Partner with us.
